      How the score works

      The Index checks your text against a dictionary of over 100 buzzwords in six categories: AI hype, corporate jargon, marketing fluff, startup speak, LinkedIn-isms and Web3 residue. Each word carries 1–3 points, depending on how much meaning it displaces.

      The score is based on buzzword points per 100 words, on a curve that tops out at 100. So one “robust” in a long email barely registers, but a short post made entirely of “synergy” does. The same text always gets the same score, and every point is highlighted in your text.

      Your text is scored in your browser and never stored.

      The Buzzword Index is satire. No thought leaders were harmed. Probably.
